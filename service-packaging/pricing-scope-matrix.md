# Pricing & Scope Matrix

Client-safe pricing guidance (MSP internal use - not for client distribution).

| Package / Add-On | Scope | Typical monthly | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | endpoints + alerts + scorecard | $X/endpoint | min 10 endpoints |
| Standard | Starter + flows + vuln scans + IR support | $X/endpoint | min 25 endpoints |
| Vuln Management | scans + remediation verify + review | flat or per-asset | requires scan authorization |
| Canary/Deception | canary VM/tokens + Class A alerting | flat + per-token | requires canary authorization |
| IR Readiness | case mgmt + evidence hunts + on-call | flat + per-incident | retainer model |
| Scorecard only | monthly report | flat | standalone reporting |

## Scope guardrails

- Endpoint counts audited quarterly (agent count = billed count).
- Scanning: internal assets monthly, appliances quarterly, cloud per agreement.
- On-call: P1 only; P2-P4 next business day.
- Add-ons billed per active scope (tokens, targets, retainers).

## Notes

- Prices intentionally left as variables - MCT management sets rates.
- Volume discounts at 50/100/250 endpoints.
