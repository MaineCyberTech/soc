# Client Deliverables

## Standard (all packages)

| Deliverable | Cadence |
|---|---|
| Monthly security scorecard | monthly |
| Alert notifications (P1/P2) | real-time per escalation matrix |
| Vulnerability review (Standard+) | monthly |
| Posture review (Standard) | quarterly |
| Incident summary | per incident + monthly aggregate |

## Format

- Client-safe Markdown/PDF (no internal tooling details).
- Executive summary first, technical detail appendix.
- Classified CLIENT CONFIDENTIAL.

## Channels

- Encrypted email or client portal (per intake form).
- Scorecard recipient per intake.

## Quality bar

- No internal secrets, no stack internals, no other-client data.
- Metrics must be accurate (queried live or clearly sample-labeled).
